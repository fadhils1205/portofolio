## Overview

The **Digitalized Master Budget System** is an internal web platform created to replace fragmented spreadsheet-based annual budget planning, multi-stage approvals, and expense tracking across departments.

## Problem

- Consolidating departmental budgets took weeks due to inconsistent spreadsheet templates and back-and-forth email revisions.
- Department heads lacked real-time visibility into remaining budget allocations when reviewing new expense requests.
- Multi-tier approval histories were difficult to trace during internal financial audits.

## Solution

Built a centralized **Laravel** and **MySQL** web application that standardizes budget cost centers, automates multi-level approval workflows, and visualizes budget-versus-actual variance in real time.

## Key Features

- **Structured Budget Planning:** Standardized cost-center and monthly allocation inputs with automated ceiling validation.
- **Multi-Tier Approval Workflow:** Role-based sign-off pipeline from section leads and department managers to finance controllers.
- **Realization & Variance Tracking:** Automated calculation of budget utilization and variance percentages.
- **Executive Analytics Dashboard:** Interactive **Chart.js** visualizations of departmental spending trends.

## Technical Architecture

Engineered with **Laravel** and **MySQL** using transactional database operations. All budget revisions and approval transitions are stored in immutable audit tables to preserve historical accuracy.

## Results & Impact

- Cut annual budget consolidation time by **60%**.
- Prevented over-budget allocations through automated threshold checks during submission.
- Established a clear, auditable digital approval trail across all departments.
