## Overview

The **Stock Opname OCR Monitoring System** is an internal web application designed to accelerate physical warehouse inventory counting and **ERP** reconciliation through automated label scanning.

## Problem

- Physical stock counts were recorded manually on paper and re-entered into spreadsheets, introducing frequent transcription errors.
- Reconciling thousands of spare-part SKUs took several days before discrepancy reports could be finalized.
- Supervisors lacked real-time visibility into counting progress per rack or operator.

## Solution

Developed a **Laravel** web platform integrated with an **OCR API** pipeline that extracts part numbers and quantities directly from captured warehouse labels and automatically cross-checks them against master inventory records.

## Key Features

- **OCR Label Extraction:** Reads serial numbers, part codes, and quantities directly from mobile camera captures.
- **Automated Discrepancy Detection:** Immediately flags mismatches between physical counts and system balances.
- **Live Opname Progress Dashboard:** Tracks completion percentages across warehouse zones in real time.
- **Audit-Ready Exports:** Generates structured reconciliation reports in PDF and Excel formats.

## Technical Architecture

Powered by **Laravel** and **MySQL** to handle high-volume inventory transactions. Uploaded label images are processed via a **REST API** OCR service, normalized through regex validation rules, and persisted with full operator timestamps.

## Results & Impact

- Accelerated monthly stock opname cycles by **2.5×**.
- Dramatically reduced SKU transcription errors during warehouse audits.
- Provided warehouse supervisors with live operational visibility.
